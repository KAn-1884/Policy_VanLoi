Privacy Policy

Protecting your personal information is our top priority. This “Privacy Policy” applies to VanLoi and the information we collect and how we use it.

This information is required to complete your participation in the service as a User
  • Information collected directly when Users use the application: Users when using the Application VanLoi, Application VanLoi may collect some technical data such as: location, IP address, access duration/session, access device, information information about access device such as operating system, manufacturer, model…

The main data collection on the application includes: : Full name, gender, date of birth, email address... This is the information that the Application VanLoi is required by User to:

  • To notify Users of application updates or changes to the service provided.
  • To handle and respond to questions, complaints and feedback from Users.
  • For feedback on app usage and administration, support, and improvement of performance, User experience, and app functions.
  • To enable us to comply with our obligations as required by a competent state agency in conducting investigations, responding to questions or instructions as required by law.
  • To prevent, detect and investigate any prohibited, illegal, unauthorized or fraudulent activity.
  • To ensure compliance with legal regulations.
  • To share location, map with friends.

SCOPE OF INFORMATION USE:

  • To contact the User to confirm the service when receiving a request from the User
